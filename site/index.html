    what these numbers mean

    rainfall: designed for CHIRPS v3 at 0.05° resolution, using a 1991–2020 baseline and nearby-cell comparison.

    temperature: designed for ERA5-Land at 0.1° resolution. the tool does not invent finer temperature detail.

    rain frequency: a rainy day has at least 1 mm. a heavy-rain day has at least 20 mm. both columns show the mean number of days per month across the baseline years.

    current season: stored separately from the baseline and labeled provisional until final source data replace it.
